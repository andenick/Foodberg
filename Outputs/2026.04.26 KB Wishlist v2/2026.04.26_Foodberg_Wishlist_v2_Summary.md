## Purpose

v1 catalogued 370 scholarly works centred on commodity prices and US farm policy. v2 triples the thematic coverage while more than doubling entry count: adds food technology (long-run driver of prices), supply-chain/retail evolution (buyer side of the market), labor, demand-side / diet transition, safety & regulation, non-row-crop commodities, and water/soil — all themes a "historically minded chef" needs to understand *why* the prices in the Foodberg app are what they are. Deepens v1 categories with heavy 2020–2026 scholarship covering COVID shocks, the fertilizer crisis, Russia–Ukraine, avian flu in poultry and dairy, GLP-1 demand restructuring, Kroger–Albertsons, and more.

## Gap Analysis (v3 candidates)

What v2 still under-covers:
1. **Non-English scholarship** — Chinese, Spanish, French, Portuguese. Requires translation/transliteration effort.
2. **Corporate 10-Ks and investor reports** — Tyson, JBS, Cargill, ADM, Bunge, Dreyfus, Nestle, Unilever, PepsiCo, Mondelez, Sysco (v2 includes a few; systematic pass deferred to v3).
3. **Culinary history / cookbook as historical source** — Fannie Farmer, Escoffier, James Beard, Julia Child, Harold McGee. Potential new Cat 26: Culinary History.
4. **Trade-press data services** — Urner Barry, Mintec, S&P Platts, Argus Media. Reference pointers only.
5. **Podcasts / long-form journalism** — *Gastropod*, *The Sporkful*, *NYT Food Investigates*. Not scholarly; deferred.
6. **Foodservice chain financials** — QSR equity research (McDonald's, Starbucks, Chipotle, Yum!, Darden) — deferred.
7. **Aquaculture economics deeper** — Cat 23 has 7 seafood entries; could be its own category.
8. **Indigenous and colonial food systems** — only 4 Colonial entries. Tribal ag, Native American food sovereignty literature missing.
