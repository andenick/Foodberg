## New Platforms for v2

### GAO (Government Accountability Office)
- Reports: https://www.gao.gov/reports-testimonies
- Relevant for: Cat 21 (Food Safety), Cat 22 (SNAP oversight), Cat 5 (meatpacking oversight)
- Always free; search by report number (e.g., `GAO-22-105398`).

### CBO (Congressional Budget Office)
- Portal: https://www.cbo.gov/
- Relevant for: Cat 2 (Farm Bill baselines), Cat 22 (SNAP cost projections)
- Reports always free.

### USDA APHIS (Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service)
- Avian influenza portal: https://www.aphis.usda.gov/livestock-poultry-disease/avian/avian-influenza
- H5N1 dairy cattle: https://www.aphis.usda.gov/livestock-poultry-disease/avian/avian-influenza/hpai-detections/livestock
- Situation reports and outbreak data — essential for Cat 5 avian flu entries.

### EPA
- Regulations.gov for rule-making dockets: https://www.regulations.gov/
- Pesticide program: https://www.epa.gov/pesticides
- RFS program: https://www.epa.gov/renewable-fuel-standard-program
- Relevant for Cat 21 (pesticide reg), Cat 2 & 12 (biofuels).

### FDA
- FSMA portal: https://www.fda.gov/food/food-safety-modernization-act-fsma
- Reports and regulations.

### USDA FNS (Food and Nutrition Service)
- SNAP data: https://www.fns.usda.gov/pd/supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program-snap
- School Lunch: https://www.fns.usda.gov/pd/national-school-lunch-program
- WIC: https://www.fns.usda.gov/pd/wic-program
- Thrifty Food Plan 2021 report: https://www.fns.usda.gov/tfp
- All reports and datasets free.

### USDA FAS (Foreign Agricultural Service)
- Country reports (GAIN reports): https://www.fas.usda.gov/data
- Oilseeds World Markets & Trade: monthly PDF, free
- Livestock and Poultry World Markets & Trade: monthly
- Dairy World Markets & Trade: monthly

### USDA AMS (Agricultural Marketing Service)
- Cattle Contracts Library (2023–): https://www.ams.usda.gov/services/government-programs
- Market News reports (daily): https://www.ams.usda.gov/market-news

### USDA ARS (Agricultural Research Service)
- TEKTRAN database: https://tellus.ars.usda.gov/
- Free access to ARS scientific publications — essential for Cat 15 (food tech) and Cat 24 (R&D institutions).

### USDA NRCS (Natural Resources Conservation Service)
- National Resources Inventory: https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/resources/data-and-reports/national-resources-inventory
- Soil surveys: https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/resources/data-and-reports
- Relevant for Cat 25 (water/soil).

### FTC
- Merger challenges (Kroger–Albertsons filings): https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings
- Press releases and litigation documents — free.

### Fed regional Ag Letters
- Kansas City Fed AgLetter: https://www.kansascityfed.org/agriculture/
- Chicago Fed AgLetter: https://www.chicagofed.org/publications/agletter/index
- Dallas Fed Ag Survey: https://www.dallasfed.org/research/surveys

### farmdoc daily (U Illinois)
- Daily ag-econ commentary: https://farmdocdaily.illinois.edu/
- Searchable archive; all free
- Key authors: Irwin, Schnitkey, Zulauf, Good

### Good Food Institute (GFI)
- State of Industry reports: https://gfi.org/resource/plant-based-state-of-the-industry-report/
- Essential for Cat 15 alt-protein economics (plant-based, cultivated, fermentation)

### PPIC (Public Policy Institute of California)
- Water and agriculture portal: https://www.ppic.org/topic/water/
- SGMA and California drought reports — free, well-curated
- Hanak-era publications especially

### Our World in Data
- Meat/dairy production: https://ourworldindata.org/meat-production
- Food prices: https://ourworldindata.org/food-prices
- Licensed CC-BY; use as data source + visualization reference.

### Nature Food (journal)
- Launched 2020: https://www.nature.com/natfood/
- Increasingly the flagship for global food-system science
- Many articles open-access; paywalled otherwise (use ILL)

### CSIS (Center for Strategic and International Studies) — Food Security Program
- https://www.csis.org/programs/global-food-and-water-security-program
- Welsh-led food security work; policy briefs on recent geopolitical shocks

### IPES-Food
- https://ipes-food.org/
- Civil-society flagship reports on food system transformation

### HLPE (High-Level Panel of Experts, UN CFS)
- https://www.fao.org/cfs/cfs-hlpe
- UN Committee on World Food Security expert reports — free, high-level

### MeasuringWorth
- https://www.measuringworth.com/
- Historical prices, purchasing power, wage data — essential for Cat 3 & Cat 14 pre-1900 context

### Brookings / Hamilton Project
- https://www.hamiltonproject.org/
- Cat 20, 22 policy briefs (Ziliak, Schanzenbach)

### AEI (American Enterprise Institute)
- https://www.aei.org/
- Smith/Glauber/Goodwin *Agricultural Policy in Disarray* and related critiques

### Broker / investor research (paid; ILL only)
- Morgan Stanley Research, Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, Barclays — relevant for GLP-1 commentary and commodity equity research. Usually behind professional paywalls; university ILL often can't obtain.
- Use if you have Bloomberg Terminal / Refinitiv access; otherwise cite headline conclusions from news reports.

### Trade-press / news that acts as primary source for recent events
- Reuters Commodities: https://www.reuters.com/markets/commodities/
- Financial Times Agriculture: https://www.ft.com/agriculture
- Bloomberg (Terminal-gated largely)
- *Progressive Grocer*, *Nation's Restaurant News* (Cat 13, 18)
- Use these for event citations; not the scholarly backbone but useful for recent-event references.

## New Bulk-Acquisition Tactics

### Good Food Institute — 3 State-of-Industry annual PDFs
One download page per year covers plant-based, cultivated, fermentation. Hit https://gfi.org/resources once per year — bulk-grab all three.

### USDA APHIS situation-report RSS
RSS feed available for HPAI updates — subscribe for live acquisition as new entries emerge.

### CBO + GAO RSS + email subscriptions
Both maintain subject-matter RSS. Subscribe to "Agriculture" for ongoing automatic updates.

### farmdoc daily
Monthly archive dump: https://farmdocdaily.illinois.edu/ — scrape by month; one session bulk-grabs ~80 articles relevant to v2.

### Nature Food TOC scan 2020–2026
Full TOC of every issue since launch → identify high-priority articles. Roughly 400 articles total; filter to Foodberg-relevant ~100.
