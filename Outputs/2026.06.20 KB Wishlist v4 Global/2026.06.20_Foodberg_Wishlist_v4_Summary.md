## Gap Acknowledgment

v4 ships as **v4-lite** per plan §16 (alternative). It under-delivers vs. the aspirational 3,200-entry target in three specific ways:

1. **Per-category depth**: several new cats have 8-12 entries rather than the 15-floor target. Fixable in v4.1 via ~200 additional entries.
2. **Non-English scholarship**: only ~77 non-English entries vs. 125 target. Expansion via dedicated non-English-source sweep in v4.1.
3. **Primary-source archives**: ~70 primary-source entries vs. 120 target. Needs systematic NYPL/LoC/Schlesinger sweep.

These are content-expansion items; the schema, structure, and generator are fully v4-compliant and ready to absorb additions.
