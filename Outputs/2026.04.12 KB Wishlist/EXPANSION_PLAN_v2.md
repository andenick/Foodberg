## 1. Context — Why v2

v1 delivered 370 scholarly entries across 14 categories, heavily weighted toward **commodity prices** and **US farm policy**. It is publishable as a wishlist of *economic* literature on food prices. But Foodberg's mission — giving historically minded chefs the full historical/environmental context of the prices they see — exposes six serious gaps:

1. **Food technology has no category**. Mechanization, biotech, processing, preservation, refrigeration, precision ag, vertical farming, alt-protein — all absent. Yet technology is the single biggest long-run driver of food prices.
2. **Supply-chain / logistics infrastructure** thin. Rail, trucking, container shipping, cold chain, distribution consolidation — each transformed what chefs pay.
3. **Demand side** thin. Engel's Law, income elasticities, diet shift, obesity economics, meat consumption trajectories.
4. **Labor history** missing. Bracero, UFW, farmworker wages, meatpacking labor, restaurant labor — all shape food costs.
5. **Retail / grocery evolution** missing. A&P → Walmart → Whole Foods → Amazon — the buyer side of commodity markets chefs negotiate with.
6. **Regulatory / safety history** thin. Pure Food & Drug Act, FDA, USDA inspection, HACCP, FSMA — each bent the cost curve.

v2 adds categories for those gaps AND deepens the strongest v1 categories with post-2020 scholarship (COVID supply shocks, 2022 fertilizer crisis, avian flu eggs spike, GLP-1 demand shift, plant-based retreat, China de-risking).

## 5. Schema Changes

### CSV additions (v1 → v2)

Add **2 new columns** to the CSV:
- `Era` ∈ {pre-1900, 1900–1945, 1945–1980, 1980–2010, 2010–2020, 2020–present, spanning}
- `Geography` ∈ {US, US+Global, Global, Europe, Asia, LatAm, Africa, Historical-Colonial}

Rationale: chef-facing consumers of this KB want to filter by "modern US stuff" vs. "deep history" and by "global vs. US." Era also lets us sanity-check recency coverage.

**Result: CSV becomes 19 columns.**

### JSON additions

- `era` (mirror)
- `geography` (mirror)
- `themes` (array, multi-tag): any of `{supply, demand, technology, policy, labor, climate, trade, finance, methodology}` — for flexible query.
- `added_in_version` (`"v1"` or `"v2"`) — lets readers see the diff.

### New priority level

Introduce **`FLAGSHIP`** above CRITICAL — reserved for the single-best canonical work per category. Cap at ~14 (one per category). Everything else stays CRITICAL/HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW. This lets a chef-user or acquisition-bot say "just give me one book per category" and get a high-signal starter pack.

## 9. Verification (v2)

On completion:

1. **Entry count** ∈ [750, 900] (target 800).
2. **Schema**: every row has 19 columns; every entry has `era`, `geography`, `themes` populated.
3. **Era coverage**: ≥5% entries in each era bucket; ≥25% in `2010–2020` + `2020–present` combined (ensures recent-trend coverage).
4. **Geography coverage**: ≥65% US-primary (`US` + `US+Global`); ≥15% foreign or historical-colonial (global comparator depth).
5. **Theme distribution**: every theme tag has ≥30 entries (no orphan themes).
6. **Category floors**: every category (1–25) has ≥15 entries; every new category (15–25) has ≥15.
